# Publication risk review

- No private email bodies, email addresses, draft messages, or unrelated notebooks are included.
- No record-board images or HTML are redistributed.
- Exact certificates are separated from numerical local-minimum/rendering data.
- Official, submitted, and internal states are not conflated.
