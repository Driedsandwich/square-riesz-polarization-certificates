# Claim boundaries

## Certified

For each listed exact finite-decimal configuration, the included branch-and-bound verifier establishes the stated continuous lower bound over the unit square.

## Not certified

- Global optimality among all configurations.
- Exhaustive worldwide novelty.
- Formal completeness of the numerically enumerated local-minimum set.
- Official-record or endorsement status for internal configurations.

The two verifier methods use distinct rigorous second-order remainder bounds but share the overall rational branch-and-bound architecture.
